About Me

I’m Ayeni Paul, a cybersecurity professional with over 5 years of experience developing practical skills in cybersecurity, with a focus on penetration testing, Security Operations (SOC), and digital forensics.
My approach to cybersecurity is rooted in hands-on learning and practical problem-solving. I have experience working with penetration testing labs, web application security testing, network scanning, and vulnerability assessment, using industry-relevant tools and techniques to identify security weaknesses and understand how systems can be better protected. Beyond technical practice, I have gained experience through cybersecurity internships and training, and I also work as a Cybersecurity Trainer, helping others develop their understanding of cybersecurity concepts, tools, and practical security techniques.
My academic background is in Electrical and Electronics Technology (B.Tech.) from the Federal University of Technology, Minna (FUTMinna). This technical foundation has strengthened my analytical and problem-solving abilities and complements my continued development in cybersecurity.
I am particularly interested in understanding how attacks happen, how vulnerabilities can be discovered and investigated, and how organizations can build stronger defenses. My long-term goal is to become a highly skilled cybersecurity professional capable of contributing across offensive security, security operations, and digital forensics, while continuously adapting to the evolving threat landscape.
I’m committed to continuous learning, practical experimentation, and becoming exceptionally good at securing digital systems and investigating security incidents.
